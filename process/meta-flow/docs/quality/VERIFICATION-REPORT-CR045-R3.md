## New finding

### `CR045-F-004-R3` — HIGH — BLOCKED rejects legitimate authorization and workflow-health stop causes

R3 introduced `FAILURE_STOP_REASONS` and maps both `FAIL` and `BLOCKED` exclusively to `stop_reason=blocked`. Direct isolated fixtures produce:

```text
BLOCKED / authorization_required
  -> CP7 decision=BLOCKED must leave matching stop_reason=blocked

BLOCKED / workflow_health_threshold
  -> CP7 decision=BLOCKED must leave matching stop_reason=blocked
```

The workflow protocol explicitly recognizes `authorization_required` and `workflow_health_threshold` as terminal stop reasons. A CP7 can legitimately be `BLOCKED` because new authorization is required or a workflow-health threshold was crossed. Requiring the generic `blocked` reason discards the true cause, makes a valid safety stop fail consistency, and pressures the orchestrator to falsify state. This is a governance and authorization-boundary regression.

Required correction: make failure-decision compatibility a set, not a one-to-one string. At minimum, `BLOCKED` must accept `blocked`, `authorization_required`, and `workflow_health_threshold`; narrower decisions such as `NEEDS_REWORK` and `NEEDS_DESIGN_CLARIFICATION` should retain their matching reasons. Add positive and negative fixtures for these combinations.
